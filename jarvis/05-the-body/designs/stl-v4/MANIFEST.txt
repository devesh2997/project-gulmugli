VESPER ENCLOSURE V4 — PRINT-SHOP HANDOFF BUNDLE
================================================

This folder is a self-contained handoff for the FDM print shop
plus a laser-cut shop. Pair with PRINT_SHOP_BRIEF.md (in this
folder) for the full job spec.

FILES (3 STL + 1 DXF)
---------------------

  jarvis-frame-v4.stl          1×  PETG matte black     ~340g  ~14-16h
  jarvis-back-panel-v4.stl     1×  PETG matte black     ~80g   ~3-4h
  jarvis-led-diffuser-v4.stl   1×  Clear/natural PLA    ~3g    ~1h
  jarvis-side-panel-v4.dxf     2×  3mm clear cast acrylic (laser cut)

DIMENSIONS (mm)
---------------

  Frame envelope:    200 × 145 × 70  (W × H × D)
  Back panel:        200 × 145 × 10  (snap-fit lid)
  LED diffuser:      175 × 12 × 4    (long thin strip)
  Acrylic panel:     ~135 × ~58       (×2 — see DXF)

PRINT SETTINGS (FDM)
--------------------

  Layer height:    0.2mm (frame, back panel) / 0.15mm (diffuser)
  Walls:           4 perimeters (1.6mm wall thickness)
  Infill:          20% (frame, back panel) / 100% (diffuser)
  Supports:        Tree, only on bottoms of internal standoffs
  Build plate:     70°C bed for PETG, 60°C for PLA
  Speed:           50mm/s outer, 80mm/s infill
  Brim:            5mm for PETG (improves first-layer adhesion)

PRINT ORIENTATION
-----------------

  jarvis-frame-v4.stl:        FRONT face DOWN on build plate
                              (gives the cleanest screen-side finish)
  jarvis-back-panel-v4.stl:   Flat side DOWN, port cluster face UP
  jarvis-led-diffuser-v4.stl: Flat (any orientation, no supports)

LASER-CUT SETTINGS
------------------

  Material:    3mm clear cast acrylic (NOT extruded — cast cuts cleaner)
  Quantity:    Cut 2× (the same DXF produces left and right panels;
                they are identical and mirror-symmetric)
  Power:       Whatever the shop normally uses for 3mm acrylic
                (typical 80W CO₂ at ~20mm/s, 100% power)
  Edge:        Polished cut edge from cast acrylic is sufficient

SANITY CHECKS
-------------

Before printing, the slicer should report all three STLs as MANIFOLD
meshes (no holes / inverted normals). If anything is flagged, please
contact the requester before continuing.

The frame fits any modern desktop FDM build volume (200 × 145 × 70mm
fits comfortably on Prusa MK4, Bambu A1/X1, Ender 3 V2, Creality K1).

QUESTIONS
---------

For tolerance / material / orientation questions, reply to the order
or contact the requester. Designs are parametric and minor changes
regenerate quickly.
