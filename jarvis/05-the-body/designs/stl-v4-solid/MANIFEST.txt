VESPER ENCLOSURE V4 — PRINT-SHOP HANDOFF BUNDLE
================================================

This folder is a self-contained handoff for a single print shop.
Pair with PRINT_SHOP_BRIEF.md (in this folder) for the full job
spec.

FILES (4 STL)
-------------

  jarvis-frame-v4.stl          1×  PETG matte black     ~340g  ~14-16h
  jarvis-back-panel-v4.stl     1×  PETG matte black     ~80g   ~3-4h
  jarvis-led-diffuser-v4.stl   1×  Clear/natural PLA    ~3g    ~45m
  jarvis-side-panel-v4.stl     2×  PETG matte black     ~25g   ~1.5h each

PRINT TWO COPIES OF THE SIDE PANEL (left + right are identical).

Total filament budget: ~470g.

DIMENSIONS (mm)
---------------

  Frame envelope:      200 × 145 × 70   (W × H × D)
  Back panel:          200 × 145 × 10   (snap-fit lid)
  LED diffuser:        175 × 12 × 4
  Side panel (each):    58 × 113 × 3

PRINT SETTINGS (FDM)
--------------------

  Layer height:    0.2mm (frame, back panel, side panels)
                   0.15mm (diffuser — finer for light transmission)
  Walls:           4 perimeters (1.6mm wall thickness)
  Infill:          20% (frame, back panel, side panels)
                   100% (diffuser only — solid for diffusion)
  Supports:        Tree, only on bottoms of internal standoffs in
                   the frame. Side panels need NO supports.
  Build plate:     70°C bed for PETG, 60°C for PLA
  Speed:           50mm/s outer, 80mm/s infill
  Brim:            5mm for PETG (improves first-layer adhesion)

PRINT ORIENTATION
-----------------

  jarvis-frame-v4.stl:        FRONT face DOWN on build plate
  jarvis-back-panel-v4.stl:   Flat side DOWN, port-cluster face UP
  jarvis-led-diffuser-v4.stl: Flat (any orientation, no supports)
  jarvis-side-panel-v4.stl:   Inside face (with dimple bumps) UP
                              — gives a clean outer surface against
                              the build plate

POST-PRINT NOTES
----------------

The side panels each have 4 small dimple bumps on the inside
face, marking centers for 12mm flat-bottom hardware pockets that
the requester drills post-print (1.2mm deep). These are not
intended to be drilled by the print shop — leave the dimples
intact as drill markers.

SANITY CHECKS
-------------

Before printing, slicer should report all four STLs as MANIFOLD
meshes. Frame fits any modern desktop FDM (200 × 145 × 70 mm fits
Prusa MK4, Bambu A1/X1, Ender 3 V2, Creality K1).

QUESTIONS
---------

For tolerance / material / orientation questions, reply to the
order or contact the requester. Designs are parametric and minor
changes regenerate quickly.
