⚠️ Critical things to verify in person before paying

1. NVMe SSD

  • ✅ Label says "M.2 2280"
  • ✅ Physical length ~80mm (about 3 inches)
  • ✅ Says "NVMe" or "PCIe Gen3/Gen4"
  • ❌ REJECT if shorter (those are 2230 or 2242 — won't work)
  • ❌ REJECT if labeled "SATA" — wrong protocol
  • ❌ REJECT if "Raspberry Pi SSD" — that's a Pi-specific bundle that doesn't work with Jetson
  • ❌ REJECT if it comes with a HAT board attached

2. Power adapter

  • ✅ Voltage: 19V (19.5V works but technically wrong)
  • ✅ Wattage: 45W minimum (65W or 90W is fine)
  • ✅ Pin size: 5.5mm × 2.5mm (Toshiba/ASUS-style)
  • ❌ 20V — too high, may damage Jetson
  • ❌ Acer (5.5×1.7), Dell (7.4×5.0), HP (4.5×3.0) — wrong fit
  • ❌ USB-C charger — Jetson uses barrel jack

3. DisplayPort → HDMI adapter

  • ✅ DisplayPort male connector on one end (NOT Mini DisplayPort)
  • ✅ HDMI female on the other end
  • ✅ Passive adapter is fine (cheaper than active)

4. Cooling fan

  • ✅ Size: 40×40mm square
  • ✅ Voltage: 5V
  • ✅ Connector: 4-pin preferred (PWM speed control)
  • ❌ 12V — won't work with Jetson's fan header

🚫 What NOT to buy

  • ❌ Raspberry Pi-branded SSD — it's bundled with a HAT designed for Pi 5, not Jetson
  • ❌ M.2 2230 or 2242 SSDs — wrong physical size for Jetson dev kit
  • ❌ Micro HDMI cable — Jetson uses DisplayPort, not Micro HDMI (that's a Raspberry Pi thing)
  • ❌ Raspberry Pi Active Cooler — Pi 5 specific, won't fit Jetson
  • ❌ USB-C power adapter — Jetson uses 19V barrel jack
  • ❌ Lenovo 20V chargers — voltage too high, may damage Jetson
