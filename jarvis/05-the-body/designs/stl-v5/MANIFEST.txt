VESPER ENCLOSURE V5 — PRINT-SHOP HANDOFF BUNDLE
================================================

This folder is a self-contained handoff for a single print shop.
Pair with PRINT_SHOP_BRIEF.md (in this folder) for the full job
spec.

FILES (5 STL)
-------------

  jarvis-frame-v5.stl              1×  PETG matte black     ~340g  ~14-16h
  jarvis-back-panel-v5.stl         1×  PETG matte black     ~75g   ~3-4h
  jarvis-led-diffuser-v5.stl       1×  Clear/natural PLA    ~3g    ~45m
  jarvis-side-panel-left-v5.stl    1×  PETG matte black     ~22g   ~1.5h
  jarvis-side-panel-right-v5.stl   1×  PETG matte black     ~17g   ~1.5h

The two side panels are DIFFERENT — print each one once. The
right panel has a port aperture for cable access; the left is
solid.

Total filament budget: ~460g.

DIMENSIONS (mm)
---------------

  Frame envelope:      200 × 145 × 70   (W × H × D)
                       feet drop 10mm below base
  Back panel:          200 × 145 × 2.5
  LED diffuser:        174.7 × 11.7 × 4   (0.3mm clearance vs slot)
  Side panel (each):   63.2 × 112.7 × 3   (0.3mm clearance vs opening)

PRINT SETTINGS (FDM)
--------------------

  Layer height:    0.2mm (frame, back panel, side panels)
                   0.15mm (LED diffuser — finer for light transmission)
  Walls:           4 perimeters (1.6mm wall thickness)
  Infill:          20% (frame, back panel, side panels)
                   100% (LED diffuser only)
  Supports:        Tree, only on bottoms of internal standoffs.
                   Side panels and LED diffuser need NO supports.
  Build plate:     70°C bed for PETG, 60°C for PLA
  Speed:           50mm/s outer, 80mm/s infill
  Brim:            5mm for PETG (improves first-layer adhesion)

PRINT ORIENTATION
-----------------

  jarvis-frame-v5.stl:                FRONT face DOWN on build plate.
                                      Feet point UP during print, no
                                      supports needed.
  jarvis-back-panel-v5.stl:           Flat side DOWN, standoffs UP.
  jarvis-led-diffuser-v5.stl:         Flat (any orientation, no supports).
  jarvis-side-panel-left-v5.stl:      INSIDE FACE DOWN (washer-pocket
                                      side against the build plate).
  jarvis-side-panel-right-v5.stl:     INSIDE FACE DOWN. The port
                                      aperture is a clean through-hole.

POST-PRINT NOTES
----------------

  - Screen mounting: the frame has 4 standoffs on the inside of the
    front face for the 7" LCD. Drill 4mm holes 4mm deep into each
    standoff for M3 brass inserts; press the inserts in with a
    soldering iron.
  - Speaker mounting: the bottom plate has a 50mm circular aperture
    surrounded by a 56mm OD × 6mm tall mounting ring. Drill 3-4
    M3 holes around the ring to bolt the speaker driver flange.
  - Magnet pockets: the frame has 4 magnet pockets (6mm dia × ~3mm
    deep) at the front corners of each side opening (8 total).
    Press-fit N42 6×3mm disc magnets into each pocket at assembly.
  - Side panels: each panel has 2 washer pockets at the front edge
    matching the magnet pockets in the frame. Glue 12mm steel
    washers into the pockets to engage with the magnets.
